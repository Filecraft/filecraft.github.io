Filecraft / Field guide

A product with
a narrower promise.

Filecraft exists to close the gap between having a file and having the right copy.

By Goni Sulaiman

I am building Filecraft around local preparation and honest verification. I want the tool to tell you what it knows, what it changed and what still needs your judgment—not distract you with a feature count.

Keep the name; sharpen the meaning

Filecraft is descriptive and not exclusive. No trademark clearance, domain ownership beyond established hosting, or global naming uniqueness is claimed. Existing download URLs and user paths are preserved instead of being migrated for aesthetics.
