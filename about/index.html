About

The space between having a document and having it ready.

I’m Goni Sulaiman. I started Prepare to solve file-submission constraints. I am building toward a broader local document-readiness platform: inspect evidence, apply reversible transformations, explain failures and verify the actual output.

This is independently maintained software, not a claimed corporation or a fabricated team. The preferred next identity Preparo collides with an existing GitHub account and App Store app. Alternative naming and organization ownership are under review; no new namespace is claimed.
