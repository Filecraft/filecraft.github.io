Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about the current product.

Does it upload files?

No engine uploads. Use local storage: OS providers, browser extensions and sync clients can have their own network behavior.

Can it compress an existing PDF?

Desktop suite offers lossless stream optimization and explicitly lossy rasterization; neither guarantees smaller output. It supports OCR through locally installed Tesseract. The older image converters make new raster PDFs. The separate PDF beta copies existing pages and enforces a byte ceiling, but does not optimize embedded images or guarantee compression. Mac additionally supports HEIC image input.

Why can’t a file fit?

The requested byte limit may conflict with page count and the supported quality floor. The application reports failure instead of silently returning an oversized or truncated file.

Is grayscale lossless?

No. Color conversion and JPEG re-encoding can remove information. Review signatures, stamps and faint marks.

Is it open source?

Source is publicly inspectable under Hippocratic 3.0 core. Its ethical-use restrictions make ethical-source the accurate description, not OSI-approved open source.
