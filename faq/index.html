Filecraft / Field guide

Straight answers.

The important boundaries of local preparation and verification, in one place.

Can it make any file smaller than any limit?

No. Lossless optimization may increase size; lossy rasterization changes fidelity. A requirement is a gate, not a compression promise.

Does a passed check mean the portal will accept my file?

No. Checks apply only to the observed facts and rules you supplied. Portal acceptance and visual review remain separate.

Does anything upload my documents?

Filecraft processing does not. Hosting requests, browser/OS behavior and cloud-synced storage are separate boundaries.

Is it open source?

Current project-owned code is Apache-2.0. Dependencies retain their own licenses. Historical releases through 0.8 remain Hippocratic 3.0.

Where are the browser store listings?

Not published. Developer-mode packages and temporary Firefox installation are not marketplace distribution.

Does it preserve signatures and Office layout?

Do not assume either. Rewritten PDFs can invalidate signatures; Office conversion is text-first, not layout-faithful.
