Filecraft / Field guide

A check is not a promise.

Readiness is bounded evidence against requirements you supply.

Three outcomes

Pass
The observed fact satisfies the named rule.
Fail
The observed fact violates the rule. Adjust the transformation or requirement.
Unknown / needs review
The adapter cannot establish the fact. Unknown is never a pass.

What remains yours to review

Legibility, every page’s appearance, accessibility, content correctness, intended recipients, institutional acceptance and any signature workflow. Parsing is not complete PDF conformance validation. No health score hides those gaps.

Receipts are not certificates

A receipt records output fingerprint, mechanical evidence and requirements. It is editable, unsigned and can expose document fingerprints and requirements. Keep it private where appropriate. The verification tool compares bytes; it does not authenticate the receipt.
