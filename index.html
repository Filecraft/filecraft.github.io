How local processing works ↗

Before you start.

Is it free?

Yes. Filecraft is free to download and use. Current original source is Apache-2.0; dependencies and older releases keep their own licenses.

Will it fit any file under any limit?

No. Some files cannot be made small enough without losing content or quality. Filecraft checks the limits you set, but you still need to review the result and the destination’s rules.

Which version should I use?

The browser workspace is for PDF pages. The desktop app also handles images and text-first document conversion. OCR and media work need separately installed local tools.

Why does my computer show a security warning?

The beta Windows app is unsigned and the Mac app is not notarized. Follow your organization’s policy and do not disable security protections. You can use the browser workspace instead.
