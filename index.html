BUILT AROUND THE HANDOFF

Less “convert anything.”
More “ready for this.”

01

Know the requirements

Set your own byte limit and page limit. Save a reusable profile. A size limit is a ceiling—not a promise that compression can reach it.

02

Keep evidence with the copy

A receipt binds the prepared bytes to a SHA-256 fingerprint. Recheck a saved copy later. Receipts are editable and unsigned, not authenticity certificates.

03

Make unknowns visible

Mechanical checks do not establish legibility, accessibility, security or portal acceptance. Filecraft keeps that distinction visible instead of inventing a green score.
