Licenses and credits.

Apache-2.0 for current project-owned source; a clear boundary for everything else.

Why Apache-2.0

A permissive license with an explicit patent grant and patent-termination terms supports commercial integration, forks, SDKs and plugins without a use-policy license restriction. This is an engineering/ecosystem choice, not legal certainty.

Not a retroactive rewrite

Historical releases through 0.8.0-beta.1 keep the Hippocratic License 3.0 under which they were distributed. New source and future artifacts identify their own license. Vendor code remains under its upstream MIT/BSD/Apache/zlib or other applicable terms.
