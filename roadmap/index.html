Roadmap

Capability grows in evidence-backed stages.

Foundation

Shared document model, strict profiles, tri-state readiness evidence, immutable transformations, bounded history, offline execution and a CLI independent of UI.

Document understanding

Sandboxed PDF inspection, local OCR, malformed-input suites and explicit parser provenance. Unknown facts must never become passing checks.

Preparation and professional workflows

Reusable pipelines, verified exports, batch jobs, comparison and format adapters. Encryption and archival claims require mature libraries and independent validation.

Platforms and ecosystem

Qualified desktop/mobile hosts, localization, accessible workflows and declarative community profiles. No arbitrary executable plugin loading by default.

These are directions, not delivery dates or available features. The detailed backlog distinguishes implemented items from proposals.
