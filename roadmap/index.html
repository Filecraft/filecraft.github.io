Filecraft / Field guide

A preparation layer.
Not a feature collection.

Prioritize repeatable local workflows, requirement gates and evidence over universal conversion claims.

Current direction

The 0.9 generation joins desktop, CLI and browser workflows through personal requirements and receipts. The extension is intentionally a dedicated preparation tab, not a privileged upload interceptor.

Next decisions need evidence

Batch submission bundles, workflow replay and loss comparison are candidates—not shipped promises. Native mobile expansion and store distribution need platform qualification and maintainer account steps. No release dates, automatic acceptance or all-format roadmap is promised.
